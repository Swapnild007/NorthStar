# NorthStar 2.0 Lesson Content Audit & Compression Standard

Status: Audit protocol approved for use; lesson-level findings must be based on readable source content.
Scope: All active lessons, including the legacy curriculum and supplemental NorthStar 2.0 lesson packs.

## Objective

Reduce unnecessary reading burden without reducing conceptual accuracy, practical competence, assessment validity, or reference value. Do not optimize for word count alone.

## Audit dimensions

For every lesson, review:

1. **Structural duplication** — repeated sections or repeated learning outcomes under different labels.
2. **Textual duplication** — exact repeated sentences, paragraphs, prompts, examples, and answer rationales.
3. **Semantic overlap** — multiple passages explaining the same concept without a meaningful increase in depth, context, or skill.
4. **Assessment redundancy** — the same fact tested in Q&A, knowledge check, practice, and reflection without distinct purpose.
5. **Scope mismatch** — advanced or tangential detail interrupting the beginner path, or prerequisite knowledge assumed without explanation.
6. **Instructional value** — whether each section contributes to a measurable outcome and has enough context to stand alone.
7. **Reference integrity** — preserve definitions, safety boundaries, technical caveats, source attribution, and version-sensitive claims.

## Compression decisions

Assign each finding one action:

- **KEEP** — distinct, accurate, and useful.
- **TIGHTEN** — preserve the idea; remove repeated framing or excess prose.
- **MERGE** — combine overlapping sections and retain their unique details.
- **MOVE TO DEEP DIVE** — preserve useful optional depth without blocking the core learning path.
- **REPLACE** — rewrite unclear, stale, unsupported, or poorly sequenced material.
- **REMOVE** — only when content is genuinely redundant or irrelevant and its learning function is covered elsewhere.

Never delete a section solely because it is long. Keep one authoritative explanation of each concept, then use cross-links rather than repeating the full explanation in later lessons.

## Target lesson flow

1. Lesson purpose and measurable outcomes
2. Core explanation (one coherent pass)
3. Diagram or mental model, where it improves understanding
4. One worked example
5. One applied scenario or case, if relevant
6. Guided practice with clear deliverable and success criteria
7. Independent knowledge check that tests a different level of understanding
8. Short recap and next-step link

Use optional deep dives for specialist nuance, historical context, extra examples, command references, and standards mapping. Avoid duplicating the full core explanation in the deep dive.

## Audit report format

Each lesson gets a row with:

- Lesson ID and title
- Estimated reading burden (word count, when source is available)
- Exact duplicate findings
- Near-duplicate/overlap findings
- Sections to keep, tighten, merge, move, replace, or remove
- Proposed compressed structure
- Coverage safeguards (what must remain)
- Status: Not inspected / Inspected / Revision proposed / Revised / QA passed

Findings must quote or point to the source section and explain why it is redundant. Similar labels or card styling alone are not evidence of duplicate content. Record exact duplicates separately from semantic overlap.

## Cross-lesson audit

Compare all lessons for repeated definitions, explanations, examples, case studies, and assessment prompts. Keep foundational explanations in the earliest appropriate lesson; later lessons should briefly recall the prerequisite and link back, then teach the new application. Preserve deliberate spaced retrieval when the assessment tests recall after meaningful intervening instruction.

## Release gates

- Every active lesson has a recorded audit status; inaccessible source is explicitly marked **Not inspected**, never assumed clean.
- No lesson loses a required learning outcome, safety constraint, worked example, practice deliverable, or valid assessment evidence during compression.
- All proposed changes preserve stable lesson IDs and progress mappings unless a separately reviewed migration is approved.
- Revised lessons pass content review, duplicate scan, and desktop/mobile rendering checks.
- Report total lessons inspected, lessons revised, exact duplicates removed, overlap merges, and any remaining source-access or QA blockers. Do not claim catalog-wide completion until every active lesson is inspected.
